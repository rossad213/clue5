Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026. This notice covers the Clue5 Discord Edition website and its Discord Activity.

What we process

  • Single Player: the app stores your daily game token and local progress in your browser. Daily guesses are sent to the main Clue5 server to check the answer; practice mysteries are served separately.
  • Discord sign-in: the Activity requests basic Discord identity access to recognize participants. For Party Mode, the server validates your Discord user session and the Discord Activity instance.
  • Party Mode: when configured and enabled, the game stores the room identifier, Discord user IDs, display names, scores, attempts used, and round status in a dedicated room database. Party guesses are checked on the server and not intentionally stored as a guess history or broadcast to other players.
  • Hosting: hosting providers process ordinary technical requests, which may contain IP addresses and basic request metadata.

How it is used

Information is used to enable gameplay, resume sessions, coordinate party rooms, prevent unauthorized access and maintain the service. We do not intentionally include guess text, Discord IDs, or secret game sessions in public analytics events. We do not run advertising profiling in the Discord Edition.

Retention and control

Solo game tokens are kept locally until your browser clears them. Party rooms are configured to expire after three hours; physical deletion of expired database rows may occur later through scheduled maintenance. You can clear local progress through your browser settings and request questions or deletion of stored party records via the project owner.

Third parties

Clue5 Discord Edition runs through Discord for embedded app functionality, Vercel for hosting, and (when configured) a dedicated Supabase database for Party Mode. Their privacy terms also apply to information handled by those services.

Updates

These practices may be updated as the service develops. The date at the top of this notice indicates the current version.
