Privacy at Clue5

Clue5 is a free puzzle game. Guest play doesn't require an identity. Players may optionally create an account to sync their saved progress.

Game progress

Your daily results, guesses, clues revealed, practice history and statistics are saved in your own browser's local storage. You can clear local records from Archive or export and restore them yourself. When you choose to create an account, Clue5 stores your email address and account authentication with Supabase, and stores your game records in a row accessible only to your signed-in account. Your saved records may contain daily session tokens, answers for completed games, guess history and case dates. Guests can play without registering; their game records remain in their browser. Signing out does not automatically erase data already saved on the device. Cloud sync may be disabled in Settings. Account credentials and saved history are processed by our authentication and database provider, Supabase. Cloud data is protected by row-level security.

Daily-game session

To validate daily answers, the game sends guesses to a secure server endpoint. An encrypted session token is stored in your browser to resume the day's puzzle. This request necessarily passes through ordinary hosting infrastructure. The site does not intentionally send the text of your guesses to the traffic analytics tool.

Anonymous website analytics

When enabled, Clue5 uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and understand broad referral sources and browser/device types. Vercel describes this service as cookie-free and designed around anonymized, aggregated data. Vercel traffic analytics is separate from any Google advertising technology enabled on the site. We do not intentionally share identifiable game history in analytics events. Vercel's normal hosting security and request logs may still process technical request information such as IP addresses.

Advertising and cookies

When Google AdSense is enabled, Clue5 may display a clearly labeled advertisement on the home page. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads. Google's advertising services may process information such as your IP address, browser details and interactions with ads in accordance with their policies. Clue5 does not intentionally pass the text of your puzzle guesses or your saved case history to the advertising script. Advertising is not required to play, and you should not click ads unless you are genuinely interested in them.

Depending on your location, an advertising consent notice and options may be provided through a certified consent management platform. You can learn more about Google's advertising practices at Google's advertising information page. You can review applicable ad personalization options at My Ad Center.

External links

Links to other websites have their own privacy practices. Registration and payment are not required to play. Optional account email and profile data are used to provide saved-progress synchronization and account recovery.
