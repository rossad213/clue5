Privacy at Clue5

Clue5 is a free puzzle game. We aim to measure broad game usage without collecting player identities.

Game progress

Your daily results, guesses, clues revealed, practice history and statistics are saved in your own browser's local storage. You can clear records from Statistics or export and restore them yourself. Clue5 does not currently provide online player accounts or sync this history across devices.

Daily-game session

To validate daily answers, the game sends guesses to a secure server endpoint. An encrypted session token is stored in your browser to resume the day's puzzle. This request necessarily passes through ordinary hosting infrastructure. The site does not intentionally send the text of your guesses to the traffic analytics tool.

Anonymous website analytics

When enabled, Clue5 uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and understand broad referral sources and browser/device types. Vercel describes this service as cookie-free and designed around anonymized, aggregated data. We do not add a separate advertising tracker or share identifiable game history in analytics events. Vercel's normal hosting security and request logs may still process technical request information such as IP addresses.

External links

Links to other websites have their own privacy practices. The game itself does not require registration or payment.
