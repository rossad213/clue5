How to Play Clue5

Five clues. One mystery. Clue5 is a free daily detective-style guessing game. Discover a hidden answer from five clues that become progressively more specific.

The rules

  1. Read the first clue in your case file.
  2. Enter your guess. You have up to six total incorrect guesses before the case closes.
  3. If you need help, reveal another clue. Revealing clues does not use a guess.
  4. Guess the answer as early as possible to earn more detective points. The first clue earns 100 points, the fifth 20.
  5. Finish the daily mystery, review your case report and return the next day.

What can the answers be?

Each mystery can be about a real person, country, place, invention, song, movie, TV show, historic event, animal, scientific idea, food or another well-known subject. We accept curated alternate names and close spelling variations, including everyday equivalents such as car for automobile.

Is Clue5 free?

Yes. You can play the daily mystery and the unlimited practice archive for free without creating an account.

Does practice spoil upcoming daily puzzles?

No. The practice archive uses a separate collection of mysteries. Future daily answers are reserved and checked by a server, so opening unlimited practice does not reveal them.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Clue5 works on desktop and mobile, and can be added to your home screen as a progressive web app. Previously loaded practice content is available offline. Daily mysteries require an internet connection.

Where are my statistics saved?

Your guesses, calendar history and statistics are saved in your browser. You can export and restore a backup from the Statistics screen. Optional accounts support cloud synchronization across devices when you sign in and enable sync in Settings; guest records remain on the device. Cloud sync depends on the account service being available.
